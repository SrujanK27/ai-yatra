AI YATRA: CHALUKYAN HERITAGE LENS

AI-Based Intelligent Multimodal Tourist Guide for Rock-Cut Heritage Monuments

Theme: Smart Technology Domain: Computer Vision & Multimodal AI Academic Year: 2025–2026
01. Problem Statement

The 6th–8th century Chalukyan rock-cut and structural monuments across Badami, Pattadakal, and Aihole represent the cradle of Indian temple architecture. However, modern tourists face major barriers: a severe shortage of certified multilingual guides, static on-site signboards lacking contextual depth, complete inability to interpret classical stone epigraphs (Halegannada / Sanskrit), and lack of instant, hands-free audio-visual narration.

02. Project Objectives
  • Instant Vision Recognition: Real-time stone carving & facade classification via live camera capture.
  • Bilingual Accessibility: Complete English & Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ) toggle across audio guides, details, and UI.
  • Epigraphical Deciphering: Transliteration and historical translation of ancient inscriptions.
  • Zero-Install Web App: Client-side optimized PWA requiring no app store downloads.
03. Proposed Solution

AI Yatra implements an intelligent, privacy-first web architecture that captures monument images, compresses them in-browser via HTML5 Canvas, processes visual features using Google Gemini 2.5 Flash Multimodal Vision, verifies against a calibrated ASI Epigraphical Dataset, and delivers rich multimedia insights including narration and interactive AI assistance.

08. Conclusion

AI Yatra successfully demonstrates how modern Multimodal Generative AI and lightweight web technologies can bridge ancient cultural heritage with tourists. By providing instant in-browser visual recognition, epigraphical transliteration, and localized audio narration with zero hardware dependency, the project solves the critical guide shortage while preserving the historical narrative of Chalukyan architecture.
